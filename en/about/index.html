About PhysioArt

Physiotherapy with time, attention and a plan

PhysioArt is a rehabilitation and physiotherapy centre in Sfântu Gheorghe. We work with adults, children, athletes and older people who want to move without pain and with confidence.

Who we are

PhysioArt brings together physiotherapists, manual therapists and rehabilitation specialists in one welcoming centre on József Attila Street.

We treat musculoskeletal and spinal problems, sports injuries and post-operative conditions, and we put real emphasis on prevention: movement that lowers the risk of injury and pain.

How we work

Four steps, one personal plan

  1. 1

    Assessment

    A conversation about your complaints and goals, followed by a posture and movement examination.

  2. 2

    Plan

    We explain what we found and agree on a treatment plan, its frequency and realistic milestones.

  3. 3

    Treatment

    Exercise therapy and hands-on techniques, supported by device-based treatments where they help.

  4. 4

    Follow-up

    We review progress, adjust the plan and give you exercises to prevent problems from returning.

Our physiotherapists

Meet our physiotherapists

The profiles below are based on the qualifications PhysioArt has published. The wider team includes further physiotherapists and therapists.

Magyarosi Orsolya

Physiotherapist · Manual therapist · Sports rehabilitation trainer

As a physiotherapist, she specialises in musculoskeletal and spinal conditions and in sports injuries. With a dedicated spinal stabilisation programme she helps people with back pain and complaints caused by a sedentary lifestyle, and she treats scoliosis.

Through sports rehabilitation she supports athletes in their recovery after injuries and surgery, tailored to the individual and their sport. Preventive movement matters a great deal to her, as does running with good, individually adapted technique.

Szabó Kinga

Physiotherapist · Manual therapist

Her main goal as a specialist is to find the quickest route to recovery after injuries and surgery. Rehabilitation, returning to everyday life and the right prevention to avoid re-injury are at the centre of her work.

With young children she pays particular attention to the healthy development of natural movements such as rolling, crawling, sitting, climbing and standing. With adults she focuses on restoring muscle balance, treating complaints caused by a sedentary lifestyle and preventing chronic pain. Her treatment plans are always based on an initial assessment.

Areas of focus

  • Rehabilitation after injury and surgery
  • Children's motor development
  • Shoulder rehabilitation
  • Trigger point therapy
  • Scoliosis (Schroth)
