About PhysioArt

A rehabilitation centre built around people

PhysioArt helps people recover from injuries and surgery, manage back and joint pain, and move with more freedom in everyday life and sport.

Every treatment starts with listening and a thorough assessment. From there, our physiotherapists combine exercise therapy, hands-on techniques and supporting treatments into a plan that fits your goals.

Why PhysioArt

Careful, personal and up to date

  1. 01

    Assessment first

    We observe posture and movement, ask about your history and look at any medical documents before deciding on treatment.

  2. 02

    A plan made for you

    Your treatment plan follows your goals, whether that is everyday comfort, returning to sport or recovering after surgery.

  3. 03

    Recognised methods

    Our therapists train in internationally recognised approaches such as McKenzie, 3D Schroth and manual therapy.

  4. 04

    Exercise you can keep doing

    We teach you exercises and habits you can carry on at home, so progress continues between visits.
