Electrotherapy

Electrotherapy is a physical therapy procedure in which electrical current is applied to the area being treated. It is a common supporting treatment in rehabilitation.

Book this service

Choose a time in the PhysioArt app or client portal, or call us to book.

Book an appointment0754 282 934

What the treatment involves

Electrodes are placed on the skin and a gentle current is applied, felt as tingling or light muscle contractions. Depending on the settings, it is used for pain relief, muscle relaxation or muscle strengthening.

Who it may suit

  • Acute and chronic muscle pain
  • Nerve-related pain
  • Muscle spasms
  • Maintaining muscle strength and range of movement

A typical session

You rest comfortably while the device runs. It is often combined with exercise therapy.

What to expect

  1. 1We start by asking about your complaints, history and goals.
  2. 2Your therapist explains each step and checks how you feel throughout.
  3. 3You leave with clear next steps, and often a few exercises to do at home.

Before your first treatment

Device-based treatments are not suitable for everyone. They are usually avoided during pregnancy, with a pacemaker or other implanted electronic device, with active infections or fever, tumours, or bleeding disorders. Your therapist checks this with you before the first treatment.

The information on this page is general and does not replace a medical or physiotherapy assessment. Results vary from person to person.
